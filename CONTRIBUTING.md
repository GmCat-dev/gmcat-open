# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help! A few things to know:

- The `sites` folders are copies of the live sites, refreshed from our server. If you suggest a change,
  we'll make it in our own tools and it will appear here on the next update, so your pull request may be
  closed with a thank-you rather than merged directly. Your idea still counts!
- Be kind. GmCat's sites are for everyone, including kids. Keep words and pictures all-ages.
- Please don't add trackers, ads, outside scripts or anything that collects data. Privacy is the point.
- Art suggestions are welcome as ideas; we can't accept artwork into the repository.

Small fixes (typos, broken links, accessibility) are especially welcome.
