Gentle Reading View

Make any text easier on your eyes with adjustable size, spacing, line width and background.

Paste something above, then shape the page into the view that feels most comfortable for you.

🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or saved on our servers.

Good to know

Is this a dyslexia font?

It uses a familiar sans-serif typeface and lets you adjust the features many readers find useful: larger text, generous spacing and shorter lines.

Are there perfect settings for dyslexia?

No. Reading needs differ from person to person, so the best settings are the ones that feel comfortable to you.

Is pasted text saved?

No. This tool keeps the text only in the current page and does not save or upload it.
