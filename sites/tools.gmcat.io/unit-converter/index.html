Good to know

How accurate are the cooking conversions?

Cups to grams depends on the ingredient and how you fill the cup. We use common reference weights (for example, 1 cup of all-purpose flour is about 120 g, spooned and leveled). For baking that needs to be exact, a kitchen scale is best.

Which cup does it use?

“Cup (US)” is 236.6 mL, used in most American recipes. “Cup (metric)” is 250 mL, used in Australia, Canada and New Zealand. Spoons and fluid ounces are US sizes.

Are gallons US or imperial?

Both are listed. A US gallon is 3.785 liters and an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 liters.

Can I type in either box?

Yes. Type in the “To” box and the “From” box updates, so you can convert in either direction.
