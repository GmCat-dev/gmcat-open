Good to know

How are words counted?

Anything separated by spaces that contains a letter or number counts as one word, so “don't” and “well-known” are one word each. In Chinese and Japanese, each character counts as a word.

How is reading time worked out?

Reading time uses an average silent reading speed of 238 words per minute. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute, a comfortable pace for a talk or video.

Why does the X count differ from the character count?

X counts every link as 23 characters however long it is, and counts most emoji and many non-Latin characters as 2. We follow the same rules, so our estimate should match the X app closely.

Is my text saved?

Your text is kept in this browser only, so it's still here if you reload the page. Press Clear to remove it. It's never uploaded.
