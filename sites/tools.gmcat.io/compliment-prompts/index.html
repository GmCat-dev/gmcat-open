Good to know

Are these made by AI?

No. Every prompt comes from a small, hand-written list.

What makes a good compliment?

Be sincere and specific. Notice a person's kindness, effort, choices or creativity instead of making assumptions about them.

Why avoid comments about appearance?

People have different comfort levels around appearance. Compliments about character and actions are often more meaningful and easier to receive.
