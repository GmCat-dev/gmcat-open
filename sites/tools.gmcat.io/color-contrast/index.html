Good to know

What contrast should normal text have?

WCAG AA calls for at least 4.5:1 for normal text. Large text needs at least 3:1.

What is enhanced contrast?

WCAG AAA's enhanced target is 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

Does passing guarantee accessibility?

No. Contrast is one part of accessible design. Also use clear labels, readable type, keyboard support and more than color alone.
