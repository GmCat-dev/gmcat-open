Good to know

How is percent change calculated?

Percent change is the difference between the new and old number, divided by the old number, times 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase; going from 100 to 80 is a 20% decrease.

Is the tip worked out before or after tax?

The tip is a percentage of whatever bill amount you enter. Many people tip on the amount before tax; enter whichever you prefer.

What does “round each share up” do?

It rounds each person's share up to the next whole amount, so nobody has to deal with coins. The total shown includes the extra.

Which currency does it use?

Any. The calculators work with plain numbers, so they work the same for dollars, euros, pounds or anything else.
