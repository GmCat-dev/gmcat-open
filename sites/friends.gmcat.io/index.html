The Hollow

Muglight Hollow is not hidden by magic, but it is easy to miss if you are always in a hurry. The low doors are for turtles, the window perches are for parrots, and a cat and a puppy can share a table.

Every morning the first warm light reaches the old bell tower. GmCat calls that moment muglight. She opens the door of her café, says “GM”, and the whole neighborhood gets moving.

Nobody has to be cheerful. Here, GM just means: I see you, a new day is here, and we can begin together.

Kindness is a hundred small things.

Places in the Hollow

Everything is a short walk from everything else. Or a long one, if you are a turtle.

  • Common Cup SquareThe middle of everything, named for its round fountain shaped like a wide cup.
  • The Warm MugGmCat's tiny corner café. Opens at muglight. Everyone ends up here.
  • Lantern LoftA reading room above the square. The lamp is on all night.
  • Patchwork PressThe print shop where the GM Gazette is made, one dramatic headline at a time.
  • The repair shedBehind the square. Nothing in it is ever thrown away.
  • The moonflower gardenAn archway of flowers, an outdoor table and an oven that is usually warm.
  • The river pathSlow, clear water and the best place to wait out a bad mood.
  • The hill trailsEvery one of them is on a map. Somebody quiet drew it.
