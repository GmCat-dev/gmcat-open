Our principles

People before the technology.

These six promises guide what we research, write and correct.

1

People first

We cover AI that helps humans flourish.

2

Honest and sourced

Every factual claim links to where it came from.

3

Both sides

We include limits, risks and thoughtful skeptics.

4

Plain language

No hype and no jargon when ordinary words will do.

5

Free and independent

This site is free, has no ads and sells nothing.

6

Transparent

AI helps draft. A human reviews. We correct mistakes openly.

How a post gets made

AI can help find leads and prepare a first draft. It does not get the final word. A human reviews each post before publication, checks the links and looks for claims that need more context. Every weekly post names its sources and explains who drafted and reviewed it.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we will fix it and add a dated note explaining what changed. Quietly making a factual error disappear would break the trust this site is trying to earn.

What you will not find here

No ads, sales pitches or hidden product promotion. This site is free, has no ads and sells nothing. We also do not treat a promising study, a company announcement or a confident AI answer as settled fact.
